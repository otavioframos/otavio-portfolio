# Portfolio direction
Industrial editorial: expressive condensed typography, graphite surfaces, vermilion accents, precise project labels, and large original product visuals.
DFII: impact 4 + fit 5 + feasibility 4 + performance 4 - consistency risk 2 = 15.
Audience: recruiters and design hiring managers evaluating product ownership, design craft, and practical AI experience.
Anchor: oversized condensed thesis, orange final line, and numbered full-width project presentations.
Typography: Barlow Condensed for display; Manrope for readable copy; IBM Plex Mono for project metadata.
Motion: one subtle content entrance, small image movement on pointer hover, reduced-motion support. No parallax or scroll interception.
Content: based on the user's statements, LinkedIn export, existing Notion case studies, and public MindYoung site. No internal revenue, invented test results, or unconfirmed employment dates.
Assets: original public portfolio and product images, copied locally. Content Radar images are design artifacts with illustrative cards; Avela imagery is a design exploration, not verified testimonial evidence.
Draft remains private and noindex. No social-preview image requested or created.

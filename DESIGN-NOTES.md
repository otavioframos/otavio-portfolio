DFII: impact 4 + fit 5 + feasibility 4 + performance 4 - consistency risk 2 = 15.
Audience: recruiters and design hiring managers evaluating product ownership, design craft, and practical AI experience.
Anchor: oversized condensed thesis paired with a flowing monochrome field annotated with tracked regions, pale green contours, measured coordinates, and orange trails; numbered full-width project presentations.
Typography: Barlow Condensed for display; Manrope for readable copy; IBM Plex Mono for project metadata.
Motion: procedural flow field rendered with Canvas 2D, using GSAP quickTo for pointer-driven distortion. Connected-component detection measures bright regions; nearest-centroid association preserves IDs across frames. Bounding boxes, coordinates, contours, connecting lines, and trails derive from those measurements. This is a generative tracking study, not camera/video analysis or a physics fluid solver. Rendering is capped at 24 fps with a 112×124 scalar field and 1.5 pixel ratio for crisp annotations. It pauses offscreen, in hidden tabs, with reduced motion, and via its pause button. Touch keeps native scrolling; an inline SVG supplies a static fallback.
